Academy Overview

AN-NUR INTERNATIONAL CADET MADRASAH was established in 2024 at Kaptai Rastar Matha, Mohara, Chandgaon, Chattogram. It stands as a state-of-the-art educational institution combining standard English medium & cadet syllabus with authentic Quranic Hifz.

Founder Principal

Hafez Maulana Mufti Abdul Majed

"We are committed to nurturing each student with profound Islamic values, exemplary moral discipline, and high competence in English & modern sciences."

Academic Wings

1. Noorani & Pre-Cadet Wing

Age: 4 - 7 Years

Accurate Quranic phonetics (Makhraj) integrated with foundational English, Math, and Bangla literacy.

2. International Hifzul Quran Wing

Age: 7 - 12 Years

Comprehensive Quran memorization guided by certified Huffaz with international Tajweed proficiency.

3. Cadet & General English Wing

Classes: Level-1 to Level-10

Complete cadet curriculum offering English Medium academic subjects, ICT, and physical drill education.

4. ICT & Spoken Language Club

Open for all registered cadets

Dedicated computer lab practice paired with Spoken English and Spoken Arabic communicative sessions.
